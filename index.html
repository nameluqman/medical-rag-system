About Medical RAG System

This Medical RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system provides evidence-based medical information by combining:

  • Retrieval: Searching through comprehensive medical databases and literature
  • Generation: Using AI to synthesize information into clear, accurate answers

The system is designed to provide reliable medical information while maintaining transparency about sources and limitations.
